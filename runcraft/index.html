RUNCRAFT

An afk-MMORPG that lives on top of your Strava runs. You run as usual. After every run your character replays it in a lovingly ironic homage to classic MMORPGs: skills trigger by distance, intervals and pace, loot drops, levels grow, and the innkeeper has opinions about your splits.

One cycle = one run. All game actions cluster around two moments: right after a run and right before the next one. In between, the game asks nothing of you.

A run, replayed

A stylised location map: the dot runs along the trail, events pop on the map and print into the combat-style log beneath it. In the game this takes 30–60 seconds and can be skipped.

What the engine does

  • Pure function. (run, character, config, seed) → result. No network, no database inside.
  • Deterministic. The seed is the Strava activity id; the config version is stored with the run. Replaying gives the same result.
  • Workout type auto-detect. Laps, splits and Strava's workout tag propose "intervals", "tempo" or "base"; you confirm with one tap.
  • Regular runs still count. 1 km = 1 XP, half the drop chance, no resource spent.
  • Resource and pauses. Rage, Mana or Energy is full at the start; every trigger costs some. Hit zero and the character catches breath for 500 simulated metres.

Two factions

Paceliance

Hold the pace. Hold the line.

Order, even splits and a training plan taped to the fridge. The Paceliance believes a run is a promise you keep to your watch.

Hordistance

Distance is honor!

Mud, hills and long runs that end somewhere else. The Hordistance believes the only bad run is the one that fits on a treadmill.

Faction is chosen per character, and characters per account are unlimited. The faction level is the sum of the levels of everyone who runs under its banner.

Short legs, absurd cadence. A Go-nome takes three steps where others take two and finds loot under every one of them. Run at 170+ steps per minute and the drop chance rises by 10 %.

Un-dawn Before the Sun

They don't need sleep, or so they claim. Start a run between 05:00 and 10:00 and the Un-dawn's loot luck rises by 10 %; the rest of the day they are somewhat grumpy.

Humans can't jump higher or run faster, but they can negotiate. Every quest pays 15 % more coin, because someone had to talk to the innkeeper.

Go-go-blins Haggler

Everything is for sale, especially the thing you just found. Go-go-blins get 15 % more coin for whatever they sell to the innkeeper. The innkeeper stopped arguing years ago.

Three classes, three ways to run

Runrior Rage

Charge

Charge fires every few hundred metres of distance, any pace: 200 m at level 1, about 1 km at level 60. Long runs and easy miles are its home; the further you go, the angrier it gets.

Favourite style: base running, long and easy.

Runage Mana

Blink

Blink fires inside fast intervals: watch laps or auto-detected repeats, 100 m apart at level 1 and 500 m at 60, pace no slower than the threshold. Hold the pace across more reps and the mage keeps vanishing.

Favourite style: intervals.

Rungue Energy

Sprint

Sprint fires on stretches faster than the pace threshold: 150 m apart at level 1, 750 m at 60, threshold 5:30 → 4:15 min/km. Tempo runs, races, negative splits: it steals loot the moment you dip under the threshold.

Favourite style: tempo running.

Crits stack within a run and multiply the drop chance; three or more runs a week carry a stack into the next one. Every trigger drinks a little Rage, Mana or Energy; run out and the character catches breath for 500 simulated metres.

What the innkeeper does

  • Guaranteed gear. One piece per class in every level bracket, always a step below the best drop of your location. Saved up? Bought.
  • The daily deal. One random item from the location pool, sometimes Rare or Epic, swapped at midnight your time.
  • Consumables. The resource potion of your class, the elixir of luck and the scroll of experience.
  • Services. Extra bag slots at rising prices, and a new nickname if the old one was a mistake.
  • Selling. Anything but quest items, at a quarter of the price. Grey junk was born for this.
  • Opinions. After release the innkeeper comments on your last run. He has seen your splits.

Three quests, always

  • Level quest. "Reach level N." Pays coins, refreshes the moment you complete it.
  • Skill quest. "X triggers in one workout" or "Y in a week." Pays XP and coins.
  • Unusual quest. Rolled from templates that read your splits, laps and elevation: negative split, metronome pacing, dawn patrol, a climbing week, an item hunt. One free reroll a day. Always pays an item, Uncommon or better.
  • Heart-rate and elevation quests are offered only if your runs carry that data. Quest chains with a story come after release.

Your data, your eyes only

  • Runcraft reads only your runs from Strava: distance, time, splits, laps, cadence and heart rate when present.
  • Other players never see your running data. They see game stats: level, skill triggers, items, faction.
  • Disconnect Runcraft in your Strava settings at any time; raw activity data is deleted.
