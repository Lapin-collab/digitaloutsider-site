RUNCRAFT

An afk-MMORPG that lives on top of your Strava runs. You run as usual. After every run your character replays it in a lovingly ironic homage to classic MMORPGs: skills trigger by distance, intervals and pace, loot drops, levels grow, and the innkeeper has opinions about your splits.

How it works

1

You run

With your watch, your laps, your Strava. Runcraft tracks nothing and never interrupts a run.

2

Strava hands over the run

Once the activity lands in Strava, Runcraft picks it up: distance, splits, laps, cadence, pace.

3

Your character replays it

A deterministic simulation: skills trigger by metres, intervals and pace, resource drains, crits stack.

4

Loot, XP, the Tavern

Five minutes after the run: sort the drops, sell the junk, take a quest, equip the epic, go run again.

Three classes, three ways to run

Runrior Rage

Charge triggers every few hundred metres of distance, any pace. Long runs and easy miles are its home.

Runage Mana

Blink triggers inside fast intervals: watch laps or auto-detected repeats. The more reps you hold, the more it blinks.

Rungue Energy

Sprint triggers on stretches faster than a pace threshold. Tempo runs, races, negative splits.

Built for serious runners

  • Thresholds are honest and grow with level: from 200 m per Charge at level 1 to about 1 km at level 60; pace thresholds from 5:30 to 4:15 min/km.
  • Reaching the level cap of 60 takes about a year for a runner doing four runs a week, half of them interval or tempo workouts.
  • Easy runs still count: a kilometre of regular running is a point of experience and a chance at loot.
  • Every curve, threshold, drop table and price lives in a config that gets tuned weekly.

Your data, your eyes only

  • Runcraft reads only your runs from Strava: distance, time, splits, laps, cadence and heart rate when present.
  • Other players never see your running data. They see game stats: level, skill triggers, items, faction.
  • Nothing is used to train AI models. Ever.
  • Disconnect Runcraft in your Strava settings at any time; raw activity data is deleted.
